The Player wants to enter the Labyrinth.

1. If an Invite is given here, use it: $ARGUMENTS. Otherwise ask the Player to paste the Invite the Sysop sent them, and wait.
2. Call `join` with that Invite.
3. Greet the Player by the name in the result, in their language, and tell them in one or two sentences what they can do: sense the place, move, touch things, speak out loud.
4. From now on, pass the same Invite on every tool call.
